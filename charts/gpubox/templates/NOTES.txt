Split MagicDNS preflight (the chart cannot change Tailnet DNS with an AuthKey):
  1. Discover the cluster DNS service IP:
     kubectl get svc -n kube-system kube-dns -o jsonpath='{.spec.clusterIP}'
  2. In the Tailscale DNS admin page, add a restricted nameserver:
     Domain: svc.cluster.local
     Nameserver: <CoreDNS ClusterIP>

Before accepting routes, verify that Tailnet subnet routes do not overlap this
cluster's Pod or Service CIDRs. Tailnet grants must also permit the required
inbound and outbound traffic.
